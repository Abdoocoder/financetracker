# Brag Plan — Fajrak (فجرك)

## What is this app?

Fajrak is the first fully Arabic personal finance manager (Jordan / GCC first). One app to know where every dinar goes, plan to pay off debts, watch investments, and reach financial freedom — all in one place, with a dark luxury "dawn" aesthetic. The tagline: *"Your financial dawn starts today 🌅"*.

## The angle

Finance apps show spreadsheets. Fajrak is a journey: from the confusion of "Where did the salary go?" to the relief of watching your financial health score rise — your personal dawn from dark to light. The video takes the viewer on that night-to-dawn arc, regionally resonant (badge: 🇯🇴🇸🇦🇦🇪, Zakat countdown, free forever, halal investments).

## Hook (first ~3.5s)

Wide, dark night-sky screen. Small teal line whispers the tease: **"Every month the same question…"** — then the question arrives in huge Cairo type:

> **"Where did the salary go?"**

A beat of silence. Then the screen begins to dawn — deep navy lifting toward warm gold-blue light as the answer fades in:

> **"Fajrak has the answer — by numbers, not guessing 🌅"**

## Key moments (middle, ~13s)

**Scene 2 — Reveal (4.5s):** The landing morphs into the dashboard. The **Financial Health Score counts 0 → 82** (green), your hero balance settles into place, `dash_subtitle` glows beneath: *"Your financial dawn starts today 🌅"*. The "numbers are heroes" principle — cold, confident numerals.

**Scene 3 — Highlight (4s):** **Zakat haul countdown** ticks toward the due date; **Quick Add** drops in a transaction in one tap. Small, satisfying, precise.

**Scene 4 — Highlight (4s):** The **5-stage financial roadmap** walks forward — Stage 2: *"Debt Payment"* with confetti when "Pay off the smallest debt first 🎯" clears; a FIRE readout shows **years to financial freedom counting down**.

## Outro / punchline (~3.5s)

Gentle dawn fully breaks. The line lands:

> **"From first transaction to financial freedom. فجرك — Your dawn."**

Logo, tagline, `Start Free ←` · `✓ Free Forever` · `✓ No Credit Card Required` · `✓ Halal Investments`.

## User flow worth showing

Landing (hero) → register (one tap) → dashboard with Health Score → Quick Add a transaction → Zakat countdown → roadmap/debt progress → freedom.

## Tone

- **Preset:** cinematic
- **Creative direction:** the arc of a dawn — the film opens in deep night-sky blues (`#070B14` → `#162032`) and gradually warms to gold-blue `#3B7EF6`/`#6BA3FF` light as financial clarity arrives. Big Cairo typography, rhythmic motion, counting metrics doing the talking.
- **Interpretation:** wide cinematic reveals — screen-scale type entrances, metric counters as the heartbeat, soft glow blooms, a single confident payoff frame. Audio: cinematic music + soft SFX for the counter ticks and dawn chime; subtle audio-reactive breathing on hero glow and the score counter.
