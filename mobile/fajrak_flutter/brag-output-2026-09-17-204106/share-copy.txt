Fajrak Flutter — Your Dawn. Your Wealth. 🌅

A native Android finance tracker built with Flutter, Arabic-first, with Islamic finance features built in. Shares a Supabase + Firebase brain with its Next.js web twin for real-time sync.

✨ 22 screens · 100% feature parity · On Google Play

Features:
📊 Health Score dashboard with live sparklines
🗑️ Swipe-to-delete transactions
🕌 Zakat Calculator — auto-filled, Hawl countdown
🔥 FIRE Calculator — Lean/Full/Fat modes
📚 Daily Islamic lessons + streak tracking
🏆 20+ achievements, 6 levels
🔔 Smart notifications with per-channel toggles & privacy controls

Built with: Flutter 3.x, Dart 3.x, Supabase, Firebase FCM, Cairo font (Arabic + Latin), Glassmorphism Material 3

Download: fajrak.com/download
