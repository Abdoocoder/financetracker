# Brag Plan: Fajrak (فجرك) — Smart Financial Management for the Arab World

## What is this app?
Fajrak (فجرك = "Your Dawn") is a comprehensive Arabic-first personal finance app that unifies banking, debt tracking, investing, Zakat calculation, FIRE planning, and a gamified 5-stage wealth roadmap — built specifically for the Arab world with regional currencies, halal investing, and full RTL support.

## The angle
**"Where did the salary go?"** — The universal Arab world pain point, answered not with generic budgeting but with a culturally-native financial operating system. Dawn metaphor: Fajrak = "Your Dawn" — every feature moves you from financial darkness to first light. The video doesn't show a dashboard; it shows the *moment the answer appears*.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen. White Arabic text types out character-by-character with subtle key ticks:
**"أين راتبك؟"** (Where did the salary go?)
→ Hard cut to: The hero gradient title "Where did the salary go?" in the app's gradient (blue → green), resolving from the Arabic. The answer lands: **"Fajrak has the answer — by numbers, not guessing 🌅"**

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The Dashboard Reveal** — Phone frame slides in. Hero Balance Card: Net Worth **١٢,٤٥٠ KWD** (green glow pulse). Monthly Income **٨,٢٠٠**. Savings Rate **٢٨%**. Real numbers, real Arabic locale formatting.
2. **Debt Never Surprises You** — Simulated interaction: Tap the "Debt Payments" card. It expands → shows structured payment plan with auto-deduction. Green checkmarks cascade down the list. Sound: satisfying "cash register" chime per debt.
3. **Wealth Roadmap — 5 Stages** — Horizontal scroll: Awareness → Debt Freedom → Safety Net → Invest & Grow → Financial Freedom. Each stage card unlocks with a subtle glow. The "Financial Freedom" card gets the gold/amber accent. Quote appears: *"The upper hand is better than the lower hand"*
4. **Zakat + FIRE + Halal** — Rapid trio: Zakat Calculator auto-fills from real data. FIRE Calculator shows "Lean FIRE in 7.2 years". Halal Investment badge pulses. No generic "AI-powered" — specific cultural features.

## Outro / punchline
Phone frame pulls back. Dark navy background. Gradient text (the same hero gradient):
**"Your finances deserve better than Excel 💡"**
→ Logo mark (the dawn icon) + **"Fajrak — فجرِك"**
→ Small: **Start free • No card • Built for the Arab world**

## Audio direction
- **Role:** Warm bed with sparse professional accents
- **Music:** `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (upbeat, corporate-but-warm, ~110 BPM) — start at 0:08 (after intro), volume -18dB, fade in 1.5s, fade out last 2s
- **Music treatment:** Low bed throughout. Beat sync only for the 5-stage roadmap card unlocks (every 2nd beat). No beat sync for number counters — they tick at their own readable pace.
- **Music cue guidance:** Using preset cues from vol-10. Strong cues at ~0:12, 0:18, 0:24 for major reveals. Beat grid windows at 0.55s intervals for sequential roadmap cards.
- **Audio-reactive treatment:** Subtle; hero gradient text presence breathes with music RMS (opacity 0.95→1.0), not waveform bars.
- **SFX posture:** Sparse; motion-matched only. Professional restraint.
- **Audio-coupled moments:**
  - Hook Arabic text types out: subtle keyboard ticks (keypress-001.wav)
  - Hero gradient resolves: single soft synth "resolve" chord
  - Net worth counter counts up: subtle counter tick per digit (interface/click_001.ogg, very quiet)
  - Debt card tap: UI tap (interface/click_002.ogg)
  - Debt green checks cascade: soft "success" chime per check (interface/select_008.ogg)
  - Roadmap cards unlock sequentially: subtle whoosh + glow pulse (interface/switch_001.ogg, low volume)
  - Final logo: single low "bong" (interface/bong_001.ogg)
- **Restraint rule:** Music never leads. SFX never overlap dialog-critical moments. No SFX on text that must be read.
