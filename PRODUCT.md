## Design Context

### Users
Arabic-speaking individuals (primarily Kuwait and the Gulf region) managing personal finances. Use context: daily/weekly on mobile, occasionally on desktop. The app guides users through a financial journey — from debt awareness to investment and financial freedom (FIRE). Users are goal-oriented and engaged with their money; they want clarity and control, not decoration.

### Brand Personality
**Three words:** Dawn, Purposeful, Grounded  
The name "Fajrak" (فجرك) means "Your Dawn" — a new beginning in personal finance. The brand promise is transformation, not luxury. It should feel trustworthy and aspirational without being flashy.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Current theme:** Dark navy (#070B14) with blue glow accents — functional but heavily "AI finance dashboard" in its current execution.  
**Better direction:** The dawn metaphor is underused. Deep midnight blues transitioning toward warm horizon tones (amber/gold near key numbers). Not a literal gradient — a tonal direction. Numbers and progress should feel like first light.  
**Theme:** Dark primary, with light mode as an equal alternative.  
**Anti-references:** Generic SaaS finance dashboards (Plaid, Mint visual aesthetic), crypto dashboard vibes, any neon-on-dark "cool fintech" look.

### Design Principles
1. **Numbers first** — Financial data is the interface. Everything else serves it.
2. **Earned decoration** — Visual flourishes only where they reinforce meaning (green for progress, red for debt). No ambient glow.
3. **RTL native** — Arabic is the primary language; all layout decisions should feel right-to-left first.
4. **Calm urgency** — Personal finance can be stressful. The interface should feel steady and confident, not anxious or aggressive.
5. **No spectacle** — Motion and effects exist to communicate state change, not to impress. Every animation should have a functional reason.
